About

A short note on who I am and what I work on.

I studied Mathematics and Scientific Computing at IIT Kanpur, with a minor in Machine Learning. Since then I've worked as an Applied Scientist across credit risk, conversational AI, federated learning, and computer vision — building the models and the training and serving infrastructure around them.

Recently that's meant a corporate bankruptcy-prediction model now used by PayU for real-time risk monitoring, and a document-review agent used by the NSE to cut review timelines from weeks to days. Outside work I read a lot of papers and try to explain them in plain language — if something here is useful or wrong, I'd genuinely like to hear about it.
